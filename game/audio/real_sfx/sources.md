# DREADING SFX audition pack — sources

Processed on the box only (2026-11-18, America/Los_Angeles). No AI generation. Nothing was copied to a user PC or wired into the game.

All clips: trim + short fades, stereo 44.1 kHz MP3 192 kbps, under ~3 s. Guns peaked near −2 dBTP; bullet cracks near −3.5 dBTP; footsteps near −18 dBTP (clearly quieter). Some close-mic Pole Position takes have a few clipped source transients; those are noted below. Spectrogram checks showed natural decay and no digital buzz tails.

## Processing notes

- Guns / cracks / most footsteps came from original 24-bit WAV members of the GDC zips (typically 96 kHz).
- Freesound clips (scar_*, some footsteps) were transcoded from official HQ MP3 previews (~112–201 kbps) of the same recordings, then re-encoded at 192 kbps. A logged-in Freesound download of the original WAV would be a higher-quality replacement for those stems.
- Reload, charging-handle, shell-eject, “quick pump,” and blank-fire takes were excluded.
- Not used (inspected, skipped): Gamemaster silenced-sniper (clipped/processed); AK-74 close burst (heavy clipping, ~8% samples); TS Sound 12-gauge slug (more clipping than the 870/SPAS); civilian “ground walk” (too loud / not boots); metal soldier walks.
