LIVE OPERATIONS / ONLINE MULTIPLAYER

DREADING

1v1 or 2v2. One contested town. Command in RTS or fight in first person.

Create a match

Send each friend their invitation. No server program or installation needed.

Match lobby

The host must enter first and keep the game open. All invited players must join before the countdown starts.

Capture and hold

Recruit squads, Retired SWAT, Suppressed Specialists, and independent marksmen, bazooka soldiers and mortar operators. Up to 30 troops per team. In 2v2, teammates share their army and credits and can each control a different soldier. Start with Recruit and SWAT squads plus 4 credits. Earn reinforcements every 20 seconds. Hold two of three objectives to score; first to 300 wins.

Select troops, then right-click the ground to move. Stand inside A, B or C for 10 seconds to capture. Press T to control a selected soldier in first person; M opens reinforcements. The 12-minute match ends with the higher score, or sudden death if tied.

Spectators use RTS pan and zoom. Browser-to-browser connections may be blocked by restrictive networks. If Commander 1 leaves, the match stops.
